# Label definitions, one per line, as `label: definition`. baseline.py reads this file.
# Kept in sync with the Label Taxonomy section of README.md.

analysis: uses a specific, checkable fact (a fee, date, stat, record or rule) to make, support or correct a point
hot_take: a confident opinion, judgement or prediction offered without any checkable fact behind it
reaction: an emotional response, joke or bit of nostalgia, where the point is how the poster feels rather than a claim
question: the post's main move is asking for information, an opinion, or something that is missing, with no declarative claim of its own
