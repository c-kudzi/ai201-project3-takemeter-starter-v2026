Yea, Italy (and Inter specifically) was the true starting point of mega-transfers, not Florentino Perez or the Premier League.

Adjusted for inflation, those Ronaldo, Vieri, and Crespo transfers are insane. And even more insane when you consider the way they changed the nature of transfers and fame.

Ronaldo's move to Inter wasnt just a big deal in terms of money, it was the first true mega-transfer in the modern sense of the word IMO. Ronaldo at that time was approaching global levels of fame, he was becoming an icon and on top of that his transfer fee was 30% greater than the previous record. The deal for him was of epic proportions in every way: fees, marketing, worldwide interest, etc.

Then 2 years later they come in and they increase the record by 50% with Vieri. What Inter did in the late 90s was insane and it all culminated in Hernan Crespo's mind-boggling transfer to Lazio.

Ultimately, the Real Madrid Galacticos eclipsed those transfers so many people have forgotten them but in terms of football history, the groundwork for the modern era was laid from 97 to 2000 by Inter, Lazio, and Italy.
