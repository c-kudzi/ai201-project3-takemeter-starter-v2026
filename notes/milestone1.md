**Community:** r/soccer (public subreddit, general football discussion — not
tied to one club, show or release).

## Candidate distinctions seen in the text

1. **Some comments bring a number the graphic doesn't show; most only repeat
   one.** "It was 35+% of total yearly revenue of RM" adds information.
   "70m in 2001 holy shit" repeats the graphic's number and reacts to it.
2. **Some comments correct someone with a rule or fact; others just assert.**
   "Those weren't records because they happened after Neymar" vs.
   "Neymar transfer fee totally fucked up the market."
3. **A large share of comments are questions or "where's X?"** — "Where's
   Felix?", "Kaka?", "Adjusted for inflation?" They make no claim at all, so
   none of analysis / hot_take / reaction fits them.
4. **Jokes and nostalgia are their own register.** "Something very nostalgic
   about seeing Ronaldo in that inter kit", "Next year you'll see JLingz at the
   top." Feeling, not argument.

## What this thread is missing

It's a reflective thread about a graphic, so there is almost no live
match reaction and few long argued posts. The next threads collected should be
a post-match thread, a tactics/stats discussion, and a transfer-rumour or
manager-news thread.
